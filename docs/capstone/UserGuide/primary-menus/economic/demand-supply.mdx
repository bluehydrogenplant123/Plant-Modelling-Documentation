# Demand & Supply

Use **Demand & Supply** to maintain demand and supply data for the current
diagram.

## Before You Start

- Save a new diagram with the main **Save** action. Economic entries remain
  disabled until the diagram has been saved.
- A fresh diagram has no Demand or Supply rows. Add rows or import them when
  needed.

## Steps

1. Open **User Tables**.
2. Click **Supply and Demand Data**. The **Economic - Demand & Supply** modal
   opens.
3. Click **Add Demand & Supply**, or click **Import** to load rows from a CSV or
   Excel workbook.
4. Enter the **Demand & Supply Name**, **Value**, optional **LB** and **UB**,
   **Unit**, and **Type**. If both bounds are present, **LB** must be less than
   or equal to **UB**.
5. If the row must point to a model variable, click **Add Mapping** and select
   its **Network**, Demand or Supply name, **Node**, **Port**, and **Var**.
6. Use **Delete** to remove an unwanted row or mapping. Use **Export** if you
   want a workbook copy of the current rows.
7. Click **Save** in the modal header.

## Result

The app shows **Demand & Supply saved successfully.** Reopening the panel
loads the saved rows and mappings. Deletions take effect after **Save**. If you
save after deleting the final row, the panel remains empty when it is reopened.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What To Check |
| --- | --- |
| The Economic entries are disabled | Save the diagram with the main **Save** action first. |
| Save is rejected | Confirm that numeric fields are valid and that each **LB** is less than or equal to its **UB**. |
| Import fails | Use a `.csv`, `.xlsx`, or `.xls` file and correct the row reported in the error message. |
