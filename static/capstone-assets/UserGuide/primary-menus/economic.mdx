# Economic Data

Use **User Tables** to maintain economic data for the current diagram.

## Before You Start

- Save a new diagram with the main **Save** action. The economic items in
  **User Tables** remain disabled until the diagram has been saved.
- A fresh diagram has no economic rows. Add rows or import them in the required
  panel.

## User Tables Items

| Item | What It Opens |
| --- | --- |
| **[Cost and Revenue Data](./economic/cost-revenue)** | The **Economic - Cost & revenue** modal for cost and revenue rows and mappings. |
| **[Supply and Demand Data](./economic/demand-supply)** | The **Economic - Demand & Supply** modal for demand and supply rows and mappings. |

## Steps

1. Open **User Tables** in the top toolbar.
2. Click **Cost and Revenue Data** or **Supply and Demand Data**.
3. Add, import, edit, map, or delete the required rows.
4. Click **Save** in the modal header.

## Result

The selected panel saves its economic rows and mappings for the current
diagram. Reopening the same **User Tables** item loads the saved data.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What To Check |
| --- | --- |
| The economic items are disabled | Save the diagram with the main **Save** action first. |
| The items remain disabled on a saved diagram | Wait for any computation-related operation that temporarily restricts economic editing to finish. |
